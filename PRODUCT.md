# Product: Stévillis Learning Hub (`learning.stevillis.com.br`)

## Register

product

## Users

Stévillis Sousa (Data Science professional & continuous learner) tracking and presenting career learning data: Courses, Formations, and Certifications.

## Product Purpose

A sleek, data-centric career learning hub and dashboard deployed at [learning.stevillis.com.br](https://learning.stevillis.com.br). Focuses exclusively on storing, tracking, and showcasing continuous education metrics (Courses, Formations, Certifications, Workload hours, Completion timelines). Complements Stévillis's main portfolio site ([stevillis.com.br](https://stevillis.com.br)).

## Brand Personality

Data-informed, ultra-refined, intelligent, technical excellence. Designed to harmonize seamlessly with [stevillis.com.br](https://stevillis.com.br).
3-word personality: Analytical, High-Precision, Sophisticated.

## Anti-references

- Generic dark mode templates with neon-pink `#ff0066` borders and heavy drop shadows.
- Cluttered SaaS dashboards with unreadable low-contrast gray text on dark backgrounds.
- Bland, default white pages with unformatted list items.
- Saturated AI-slop decorative background gradients.

## Design Principles

1. **Data Science & Learning First**: Elevate learning stats, total workload hours, progress metrics, and technical skill tags with crisp visual hierarchy and high-legibility typography (`Outfit` headings, monospaced metrics).
2. **Harmonized Slate & Cyan Palette**: Deep slate/slate-900 backdrop (`#0f172a` / `#0a0a0f`) paired with vibrant cyan (`#06b6d4`), sapphire blue (`#3b82f6`), emerald green (`#10b981`), and amber (`#f59e0b`).
3. **Structured Information Architecture**: Clear visual distinction between Courses, Formations, and Certifications, with quick search/filtering, workload indicators, and status badges.
4. **Fluid Responsiveness & Micro-interactions**: Backdrop blurs (`backdrop-blur-xl`), smooth transitions, interactive hover states, glass-morphic accent overlays, and clean mobile navigation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- High contrast body text (ratio ≥ 4.5:1 on dark surfaces).
- Semantic HTML tags (`<header>`, `<main>`, `<nav>`, `<article>`, `<footer>`).
- Focus indicators for keyboard accessibility.
- Reduced motion consideration for micro-animations.
